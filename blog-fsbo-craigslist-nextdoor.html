The Importance of Disclosure in DC Home Sales

In DC, sellers are required to provide a Seller's Property Condition Disclosure for most residential sales. This can include information about lead paint, asbestos, and other potential hazards. As a homeowner in DC, it's essential to understand these disclosure requirements and how they can impact your sale.

For example, homes built before 1978 may require a federal lead-paint disclosure. This can be a significant cost for sellers, who may need to hire a certified lead inspector and provide a lead-reduction plan.
