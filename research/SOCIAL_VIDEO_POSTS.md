## ENGAGEMENT STRATEGY

**What to Monitor:**
- Comments asking "How fast?"
- DMs asking for offers
- Shares to personal profiles
- Saves (high intent signal)

**How to Respond:**
- Reply within 2 hours
- Direct to phone or booking link
- Keep responses short + professional

**Sample Reply Templates:**

*"How fast can you really close?"*
→ "We've closed in as little as 7 days. Most close in 10-14. You pick the closing date that works for you!"

*"Do you really buy as-is?"*
→ "Yes! Fire damage, water damage, needs work, inherited—we buy it all. Fair offer in 24 hours, no obligation."

*"What areas do you serve?"*
→ "Maryland, Virginia & DC. Any neighborhood. DM us your address and we'll send a quick estimate!"

## TECHNICAL SETUP

### Instagram Reels:
1. Upload video file
2. Add captions/hashtags
3. Set "Allow Sharing" to ON
4. Schedule or post immediately

### TikTok:
1. Upload video
2. Add captions + hashtags (use different set than Instagram)
3. Set to "Everyone" for visibility
4. Post 7 PM Tuesday

### Facebook:
1. Share to Business Page
2. Add caption + link to website
3. Boost post (optional, $10-20/day budget)

### LinkedIn:
1. Share to company page + personal profile
2. Use paragraph breaks for readability
3. Add company hashtags

## NOTES

- **Consistency:** Post same video on same day/time weekly for 4 weeks
- **Variation:** Change captions weekly (don't just repeat)
- **Authenticity:** Respond to comments personally (builds trust)
- **Tracking:** Use unique phone number or UTM link for each platform to track ROI
