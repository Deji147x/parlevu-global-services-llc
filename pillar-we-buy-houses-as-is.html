What "As-Is" Really Means

When we say we buy homes as-is, we're not saying the house is worth nothing. We're saying you don't have to spend a dime to prepare it for sale. If the roof is 20 years old, you don't repair it. If the kitchen hasn't been updated since 1998, that's fine. If there's deferred maintenance, code violations, or even structural damage — we still buy it. The price you receive reflects the home's actual condition; we price repairs in from the start.

Why Sellers in Maryland Choose As-Is Sales

1. Speed. A traditional home sale takes 30-90 days from listing to closing. As-is cash sales close in 7-14 days. If you're relocating for a job, facing a deadline, or simply want certainty, cash is the path.

2. No repair costs. The average homeowner spends $8,000-$15,000 preparing a home for sale. Inspections, repairs, staging, painting, landscaping — it adds up. With an as-is sale, you spend zero. We buy the home exactly how it is.

3. No realtor commissions. Traditional agent fees are 5-6% of the sale price. On a $300,000 home, that's $15,000-$18,000 right off the top. You don't pay a commission in a cash sale — you keep all the equity.

4. Certainty. A traditional sale is contingent on inspection, appraisal, and financing. A cash offer is not. We close when we say we close. No surprises. No renegotiation if an appraisal comes in low.

5. Confidentiality. You don't want your neighbors knowing you're selling, or your landlord (if you're a tenant-occupied investor property), or anyone in your family to see your house on the MLS. An as-is sale is private. No open houses. No showing agents. Just a direct transaction.

Common Questions About As-Is Sales

Do you really buy homes with major damage?

Yes. We buy fire-damaged homes, water-damaged homes, properties with foundation issues, outdated systems, code violations, and more. If the home has walls and a roof, we'll evaluate it. Our price reflects all repairs and work needed.

What if I still owe the bank? Will you pay off my mortgage?

Yes. We work with your lender at closing. Your loan gets paid off from the sale proceeds, and you receive the remaining cash. You never pay the bank directly; the title company handles the payoff through escrow.

What if the property is in probate or contested ownership?

We handle probate sales and inheritance situations regularly. We work with executors, co-heirs, and legal teams to navigate the complexities. Probate can actually make a cash sale MORE attractive because you avoid months of court delays and realtor carrying costs.

Can I negotiate the offer, or is it final?

Our offer is strong and fair, but it's not final. You can counter-offer if you wish. We're here to make a deal that works for you. Most sellers accept our initial offer because it already reflects a competitive cash-market price.

What are the hidden fees or surprises?

There aren't any. Our offer is all-inclusive: no buyer's agent, no appraisal fee, no inspection contingency, no financing delays. You receive exactly what we offer. The only costs at closing are title insurance and Maryland transfer taxes — standard for any Maryland sale.
