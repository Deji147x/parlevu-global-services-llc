The Importance of Disclosure in Washington DC Home Sales

In Washington DC, sellers are required to provide a Seller's Property Condition Disclosure for most residential sales. This is because some homes may have lead paint or other safety hazards. If you're selling a home that was built before 1978, you'll also need to provide a federal lead-paint disclosure. Don't let disclosure issues hold up your sale - let Parlevu Global Services handle the paperwork for you.
