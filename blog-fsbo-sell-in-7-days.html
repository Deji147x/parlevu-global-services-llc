Can you actively conceal known defects in Virginia?

In Virginia, you cannot actively conceal known defects in your home. The state requires sellers to disclose any known issues with the property through the Residential Property Disclosure Statement. How fast can a home go from notice to auction in Virginia?

Virginia uses fast non-judicial foreclosure, which means a home can go from notice to auction in under 60 days. This is much faster than traditional foreclosure processes.
