Handling Lowball Offers Without Losing the Buyer

Selling a house can be a stressful experience, especially when you receive a lowball offer. As a homeowner in Maryland, Virginia, or Washington DC, you're likely eager to find a buyer quickly and close the deal. However, responding to lowball offers without losing the buyer requires a thoughtful approach.

Understanding Lowball Offers

A lowball offer on your house can be a challenge to navigate, especially if you're new to the real estate market. In practice, a lowball offer is typically 10-15% below the asking price. For example, if your house is listed for $300,000, a lowball offer might be $255,000. At Parlevu Global Services LLC, our cash home buying company, we understand that every seller is unique and deserves a fair deal.

Responding to Lowball Offers

When you receive a lowball offer, it's essential to respond promptly and professionally. Parlevu Global Services LLC responds to low offers within two business days, ensuring you don't miss out on potential buyers. In Maryland, where foreclosures can be a concern, it's crucial to be proactive in your negotiations to avoid unnecessary delays. For instance, if you're considering selling your house in Maryland, you'll need to comply with the Seller's Disclosure and Disclaimer Statement, even in as-is sales.

Countering Lowball Offers

If the buyer's lowball offer is too low, you may need to counter with a more reasonable proposal. At Parlevu Global Services LLC, we pay all closing costs and buy as-is, so you won't spend a dime on repairs. This means you can focus on negotiating the sale price without worrying about additional expenses. For example, if the buyer offers $275,000, you could counter with $285,000, leaving room for both parties to agree on a mutually beneficial deal.

Closing the Deal Quickly

One of the benefits of working with Parlevu Global Services LLC is our fast closing process. We can close in 7-21 days, compared to traditional listings which take 30-90 days. This means you can receive your payment within a shorter timeframe, allowing you to move forward with your plans.
