How Do As-Is Home Sales Work in Maryland?

In Maryland, as-is home sales are a common practice, especially for foreclosures. When selling an as-is property, the seller is not required to disclose known material defects. However, they must still provide the Buyer's Disclosure and Disclaimer Statement. This statement is required by law to inform potential buyers of any known defects or issues with the property.
