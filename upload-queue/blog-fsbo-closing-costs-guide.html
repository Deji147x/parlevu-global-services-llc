FSBO Closing Costs by State

While Maryland has specific closing costs, other states may have different requirements. In Virginia, for example, the state transfer tax is 4.5% of the sale price. In DC, the transfer tax is 5.9% of the sale price. For instance, if you're selling a $300,000 home in DC, you can expect to pay around $17,700 in transfer taxes.
