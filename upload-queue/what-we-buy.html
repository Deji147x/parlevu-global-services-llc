What Houses We Buy

Most cash buyers keep their criteria vague so every call feels like a maybe. Here is exactly what we look for, so you can tell in about thirty seconds whether your house is a fit.

Our Criteria

The five things we look at

These describe the homes we buy most often across Baltimore City, Baltimore County, and the wider DMV. Condition is deliberately not on the list.

What we buy
Property typeResidential, 1–4 units — single family, duplex, triplex, quad. Single family is the majority of what we buy.
Size of houseUp to 2,000 sq ft per unit
Bedrooms & bathsUp to 4 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms per unit
Lot sizeHalf an acre or less of total land
Price rangeBelow the FHA loan limit for the county the property sits in. Those limits are set by HUD and change each year, so we check the current figure for your specific county.
Condition✓ Any condition — this is not a filter

If your house sits outside one of these, tell us anyway. The list describes what we buy most often, not an absolute rule, and we would rather look at the property than have you rule yourself out. If it is not a fit, we will say so quickly instead of leaving you waiting.

Condition

What we will buy that a retail buyer will not

🏚️

Structural and damage

Roof damage, foundation problems, fire damage, water damage and mold. You make no repairs and do no cleaning before closing.

📋

Paperwork problems

Code violations, liens, unpermitted additions and title issues. These stop a conventional sale but not a cash purchase.

🕰️

Situations, not just houses

Inherited and probate property, divorce, relocation, downsizing, vacant homes and properties with tenants in place.
