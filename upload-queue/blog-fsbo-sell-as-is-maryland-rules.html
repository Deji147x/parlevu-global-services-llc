As-Is Sales in Maryland: What 'As-Is' Legally Means

Are you facing the daunting task of selling your Maryland home as-is? You're not alone. Many homeowners in Maryland find themselves in this situation, often due to unforeseen circumstances or financial constraints. At Parlevu Global Services LLC, we understand the challenges of as-is sales, which is why we're here to guide you through the process and explain what 'as-is' legally means in Maryland.

What Does 'As-Is' Legally Mean in Maryland?

In Maryland, when a seller offers to sell a property 'as-is,' it means that the seller is waiving their right to make repairs or disclosures about known material defects. This can be a complex and daunting process, especially for homeowners who are not familiar with the process.

For example, let's say you're selling a $300,000 house in Maryland with known water damage. In an as-is sale, you would be required to disclose this information to potential buyers, but you would not be responsible for making the repairs. Parlevu Global Services LLC would take care of the repairs and buy the house as-is, so you wouldn't spend a single penny on repairs.

It's worth noting that even in as-is sales, Maryland requires the Seller's Disclosure and Disclaimer Statement. This means that you must still disclose any known material defects, even if you're not making repairs. This helps ensure that buyers are aware of any potential issues with the property.
