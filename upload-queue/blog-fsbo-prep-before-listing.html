Lead-Paint Disclosure: A Must for Pre-1978 Homes

For pre-1978 homes in Washington DC, Maryland, and Virginia, a federal lead-paint disclosure is required by law. This means that sellers must disclose the presence of lead-based paint in their home to potential buyers. In practice, this disclosure is usually included in the Seller's Property Condition Disclosure, which is required for most residential sales in DC. By following our 2-weeks prep checklist, you'll ensure that your home is compliant with all local regulations.
