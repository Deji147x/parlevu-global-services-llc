Seller's Property Condition Disclosure

Before listing your house, you'll need to disclose its property condition. In Washington DC, this is usually required for most residential sales. If your home was built before 1978, you'll also need to disclose federal lead-paint disclosure duties. This is to protect buyers from lead-based paint hazards.

For instance, if your home was built in 1980, you'll need to disclose that it contains lead-based paint. This is a crucial step to ensure you're providing buyers with accurate information. At Parlevu Global Services LLC, we'll handle all the paperwork and disclosures, so you can focus on selling your house.
