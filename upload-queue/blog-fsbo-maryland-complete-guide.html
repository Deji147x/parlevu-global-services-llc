Frequently Asked Questions

Is it legal to sell my house without a realtor in Maryland?

Yes. Maryland has no law requiring an agent. You must still provide the Seller's Disclosure and Disclaimer Statement and, for pre-1978 homes, the federal lead-paint disclosure. Most FSBO sellers hire a title company and have an attorney review the contract.

How much does selling FSBO save in Maryland?

Skipping a listing agent saves roughly 2.5-3% of the sale price â€” about $10,000 on a $350,000 home. With an unrepresented buyer, savings can reach 5-6%, offset by attorney fees, flat-fee MLS, photography, and about two extra weeks on market.

What paperwork do I need to sell by owner in Maryland?

The Maryland Seller's Disclosure and Disclaimer Statement, a residential purchase and sale agreement, lead-paint disclosure for pre-1978 homes, your payoff statement, and a deed prepared through a title or settlement company.

Who pays transfer taxes in a Maryland FSBO sale?

Maryland transfer and recordation taxes total roughly 1.5-3% by county and are typically split between buyer and seller by contract. First-time Maryland homebuyers get part of the state transfer tax waived by law â€” your title company handles the math.

What if my Maryland FSBO home doesn't sell?

Four levers: reprice, improve presentation, add flat-fee MLS exposure, or take a direct cash offer. Cash buyers like Parlevu Global close in 7-14 days in any condition â€” many FSBO sellers hold a written cash offer as a backup while their listing runs.
