Comparing Net Proceeds vs Sticker Price

When comparing cash offers to listing net proceeds, it's essential to look beyond the sticker price. The net proceeds represent the amount of cash you'll receive after expenses, commissions, and other fees are deducted. For example, on a $300,000 sale, the net proceeds might be $270,000, after deducting 6% commission and closing costs. In practice, this means you might need to negotiate the price or consider other options to get the cash you need.

On the other hand, a cash offer from Parlevu Global Services might be lower than the sticker price, but it's often higher than the net proceeds. This is because Parlevu pays all closing costs and buys the property as-is, so you won't have to spend money on repairs or fees. In Virginia, where the foreclosure process can move quickly, acting early is crucial. With Parlevu, you can get a cash offer within two business days and close the sale in under 60 days.
