The FSBO Support Center for Maryland, Virginia & DC

Everything you need to sell your home by owner — free guides, honest numbers, and a no-pressure cash backup if you ever want one.

Selling your own home is completely doable — about 1 in 20 U.S. homes sells FSBO — but the sellers who succeed go in with the right paperwork, realistic pricing, and a plan for when things stall. That's what this center is for. Parlevu Global Services is a Baltimore-based home buyer; we built these resources because informed sellers make better decisions, whether they ever call us or not.

Start Here: The FSBO Paperwork Checklist (MD / VA / DC)

Print this. Every document you'll need before closing:

  • ☐ State disclosure form — MD: Seller's Disclosure & Disclaimer Statement · VA: Residential Property Disclosure Statement · DC: Seller's Property Condition Disclosure
  • ☐ Federal lead-paint disclosure (any home built before 1978) + EPA pamphlet receipt
  • ☐ Residential purchase & sale agreement (have a real-estate attorney review — typically $500-$1,500 in the DMV)
  • ☐ Earnest-money escrow arrangement (title company or attorney holds it — never hold it yourself)
  • ☐ Deed prep & title search (order through a title/settlement company early; surprises on title kill closings)
  • ☐ Payoff statement from your mortgage servicer
  • ☐ Transfer & recordation tax estimate — MD: ~1.5-3% by county · VA: modest grantor tax · DC: up to ~2.9% combined
  • ☐ Buyer verification — pre-approval letter (financed) or proof of funds (cash)
  • ☐ Settlement statement (ALTA/HUD-1) — reviewed 3 days before closing

FSBO by the Numbers

Straight data, no spin — cite it, share it:

  • ~5% of U.S. home sales were FSBO in 2025 — it's a minority path, but a real one.
  • 55 vs. 42 days — median days on market, FSBO vs. agent-listed. Budget the extra month of carrying costs.
  • 5-6% — total commission you can save with an unrepresented buyer; ~2.5-3% if the buyer has an agent you compensate.
  • 7-14 days — typical close for a direct cash sale, the fallback when the calendar wins.
